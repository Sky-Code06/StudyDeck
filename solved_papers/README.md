# 📝 Solved Papers Ingestion Directory

Drop your exam papers, question sheets, and quiz papers here:
- 📄 CAT-1 (Continuous Assessment Test 1) Papers
- 📄 CAT-2 Papers
- 📄 FAT (Final Assessment Test) Past Papers
- 📄 Midterm / Semester Question Banks
- 📄 Quizzes and Assignment problem sets

## How Solved Papers are Handled:
1. When you place a question paper PDF or image here, Antigravity extracts each question.
2. Complete, exam-grade model solutions and scoring rationales are mapped directly from your study notes.
3. The questions and verified answers are automatically published into the **Solved Papers** tab of your StudyDeck website.
